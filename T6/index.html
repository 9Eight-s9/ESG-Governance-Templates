📐 Risk Assessment Formula & Rating Scale
Risk Score = Likelihood (1–5) × Severity (1–5) = 1–25
Critical: 20–25 · High: 10–19 · Medium: 5–9 · Low: 1–4
Likelihood (1–5) Probability

1 — Rare: Could occur in exceptional circumstances only
2 — Unlikely: Not expected but possible
3 — Possible: Could occur at some point
4 — Likely: Will probably occur in most circumstances
5 — Almost certain: Expected to occur regularly

Severity (1–5) Consequence

1 — Negligible: No injury, minor nuisance
2 — Minor: First aid required, no lost time
3 — Moderate: Medical treatment, short-term absence
4 — Major: Serious injury, long-term health effect
5 — Catastrophic: Fatal, permanent disability, regulatory prosecution

🔴 Critical (20–25)

Immediate action required. Do not allow continued exposure. Escalate to Board / CEO. Notify regulator if exposure has occurred. Commission independent assessment within 24 hours.

🟠 High (10–19)

Priority action plan required within 30 days. Engineering controls or substitution. Senior management sign-off on interim controls. COSHH assessment mandatory. Biological monitoring if applicable.

🟡 Medium (5–9)

Management controls documented and implemented. COSHH assessment current. Annual review of controls. PPE as specified. Health surveillance if required. Included in routine H&S audit.

🟢 Low (1–4)

Monitoring regime in place. Standard operating procedures documented. Included in induction training. Annual review sufficient. No specialist controls required beyond good occupational hygiene.

📋 COSHH Assessment Requirements — When Mandatory
✓
Any substance listed in EH40 with a Workplace Exposure Limit (WEL) — mandatory COSHH assessment before use or exposure
✓
Any substance with a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) — all sections must be reviewed; assessment completed before first use
✓
Biological agents in Approved List — Group 2, 3, 4 all require COSHH assessment; Group 1 good practice
✓
Carcinogens, mutagens, reproductive toxins (CMRs) — COSHH requires additional controls; substitution must be considered first
✓
Ionising radiation — covered by IRR17, not COSHH, but overlapping register for completeness

🔢 How to Complete This Register — 5 Steps
1
Complete Organisation Details on the Dashboard. Set the revision date and appoint a Responsible Person — this is a legal requirement under COSHH Reg 6 for employers with 5+ employees.
2
Walk the site and identify all materials in scope. Use section tabs to work through each hazard category. Mark materials as Present, Absent, or Unknown for each entry — even Unknown is a valid register entry that triggers a survey obligation.
3
Complete the risk assessment. Enter Likelihood (1–5) and Severity (1–5) for each Present material. The tool calculates Risk Score and rating automatically. Note current controls and control gaps.
4
Document legal compliance status. For each material, confirm: COSHH assessment current Y/N, SDS available Y/N, competent person appointed Y/N, monitoring frequency, last review date. Use the Notes field for action items.
5
Export and review. CSV export produces a legal-register-ready document. JSON saves the full register for annual revision. Print for COSHH file, board reporting, and regulatory inspection. Annual review is legally required — set the Next Review Due date.
