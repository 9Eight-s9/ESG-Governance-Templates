📌 About This Instrument

The B.5 Circular Economy Asset Audit Sheet is designed for organisations conducting asset reviews during relocations, refurbishments, lease expiries, or annual decarbonisation programme cycles. It covers all major asset categories encountered across industries and provides: embodied carbon reference data (WRAP 2025, EPD databases), CO₂e savings calculations for each circular route, named circular economy partners by asset category, framework mapping to ESRS E5, GRI 306, BRSR Core, ISO 20400, and BS 8001:2017.

Unlike the original B.5 template (which covered office furniture only), this extended version is applicable across 8 industry contexts. Most organisations will use 3–5 sections — select the sections relevant to your operations and complete only those asset rows that apply.

📐 The CO₂e Savings Formula — Explained
CO₂e Saved = Qty × (EFnew − EFcircular)
Landfill Tax Avoided = Waste Tonnes Diverted × £126.15/tonne (2025)
Diversion Rate = (Units not to landfill ÷ Total Units) × 100
EFnew — Embodied Carbon of New Asset Baseline

The kgCO₂e embedded in manufacturing a brand-new equivalent asset — from raw material extraction through fabrication, transport, and delivery. Source: WRAP 2025 Embodied Carbon Factors, manufacturer Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), or ECOINVENT LCA database. This is what you avoid when you choose a circular route instead of buying new.

EFcircular — Carbon of Chosen Route Saving

The kgCO₂e associated with the chosen circular action: Reuse (transport only ~5–15 kgCO₂e), Refurbish (labour + parts ~15–40 kgCO₂e), Recycle (collection + processing ~20–60 kgCO₂e), or Dispose to landfill (transport + decomposition methane + landfill tax). Always lower than new manufacture except for disposal.

♻️ Reuse

Definition: Asset used again in its current form with minimal intervention. CO₂e saving: 85–95% vs new. Preferred route. Examples: desk moved to another floor, server redeployed to development environment, retail shelving reconfigured.

🔧 Refurbish / Remanufacture

Definition: Asset restored to full working order through repair, cleaning, part replacement, or reupholstery. CO₂e saving: 60–80% vs new. Examples: chair foam + fabric replacement, laptop screen/battery swap, commercial oven compressor replacement.

🔄 Recycle

Definition: Asset broken down into material streams for processing into new raw materials. CO₂e saving: 30–70% vs new (material-dependent). Steel 100% recyclable. Nylon 6 (carpets): recyclable. MDF (flat-pack furniture): difficult. Composite materials: often downcycled.

🏭 Circular Route Hierarchy — Apply in This Order
1
Reuse in-house — Redistribute to another location, floor, or department within the same organisation. Zero transport carbon. Maximum CO₂e saving.
2
Sell or donate externally — Charity resale (British Heart Foundation, Emmaus), Warp It platform, eBay Business, Green Standards. Asset leaves for reuse elsewhere.
3
Refurbish for reuse — Engage certified circular partner (see each asset card for named partners). Extends asset life 4–10 years.
4
Material recycling — Separate by material stream: steel, aluminium, copper, nylon, timber. Use certified recyclers. Obtain diversion certificates for GRI 306-4.
5
Energy recovery — Incineration with energy capture preferred over landfill for non-recyclable organics and mixed materials. Last resort before disposal.

🔢 How to Complete This Audit — 6 Steps
1
Scope the audit. Fill Organisation Details on the Dashboard. Select which sections apply to your organisation — most will use 2–4 sections. A logistics company: Sections A, B, F, G. A hospital: Sections A, C, H. A retailer: Sections A, D, G.
2
Complete an asset inventory. Walk the site or pull the asset register. Record quantity, age, and condition (1–5) for each asset category. Enter in the relevant section tab.
3
Select circular action route for each asset: Reuse, Refurbish, Recycle, or Dispose. Use the hierarchy above — apply routes in order 1→5. The tool calculates CO₂e savings per route.
4
Record circular partners. Named partners are pre-populated in each asset card's guidance. Confirm availability and capacity before committing route — partner capacity varies.
5
Review the Dashboard. Check total CO₂e savings, landfill tax avoided, diversion rate, and the top savings opportunities table. This data feeds directly into ESRS E5 and GRI 306 disclosures.
6
Export for disclosure. CSV export produces GRI 306-4 waste diversion data ready for reporting. JSON export saves the full audit for resumption. Print for FM sign-off and board reporting.
