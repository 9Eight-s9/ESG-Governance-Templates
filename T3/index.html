📌 About This Worksheet

The B.3 Emissions Inventory Worksheet provides a structured tool for calculating and recording greenhouse gas emissions across all three GHG Protocol scopes — direct (Scope 1), purchased energy (Scope 2), and value chain (Scope 3). It covers 56 distinct emission sources and subcategories across Scope 1 (9 sources), Scope 2 (5 sources), and Scope 3 (42 subcategories across 11 GHG Protocol categories).

The worksheet is designed to satisfy the GHG data requirements of all major ESG frameworks simultaneously and provides the baseline emissions data needed for SBTi target-setting, CDP Climate disclosure, ESRS E1 transition plan, and BRSR Core assured KPI reporting.

📐 The Emissions Formula — Explained
tCO₂e = Activity Data × Emission Factor (EF)
Scope 1+2+3 Total = Σ(all source tCO₂e)
Intensity = Total tCO₂e ÷ Revenue (£m)  |  Per-capita = Total tCO₂e ÷ Employees
Activity Data What you measure

The physical quantity of the emission-generating activity — litres of diesel burned, kWh of electricity purchased, passenger-kilometres flown, nights in hotels, tonnes of waste sent to landfill. Activity data must come from primary sources: utility bills, fuel delivery records, travel booking systems, expense systems, or supplier invoices. Estimated data must be flagged and improved over time.

Emission Factor (EF) Converts to CO₂e

A coefficient that converts the activity unit into kgCO₂e, accounting for all greenhouse gases weighted by their Global Warming Potential (GWP). DEFRA (UK) publishes updated EFs annually. The IEA publishes grid electricity EFs by country. PCAF publishes financed emission factors. Always use the most recent vintage (updated annually for electricity grids). GWP values follow IPCC AR6 (CO₂=1, CH₄=27.9, N₂O=273).

🔥 Scope 1 — Direct

Emissions from sources owned or controlled by the organisation. Includes fuel combustion in boilers, company vehicles, industrial processes, refrigerant leaks, and fugitive emissions from on-site pipework. Always reported as location-based (no market-based adjustment). Required: GHG Protocol, ESRS E1, ISSB S2, BRSR Core, TCFD.

⚡ Scope 2 — Purchased Energy

Indirect emissions from generation of purchased electricity, heat, steam, and cooling. GHG Protocol requires dual reporting: (1) Location-based using national grid average EF, and (2) Market-based using contractual instruments (RECs, GOs, PPAs, supplier-specific EFs). SBTi target validation uses market-based. LEED and RE100 use market-based. CDP requires both.

🌐 Scope 3 — Value Chain

All other indirect emissions across 15 GHG Protocol categories: 8 upstream (supply chain) and 7 downstream (product use). Typically 70–90% of total corporate footprint. Begin with a screening exercise using spend-based estimates for all 15 categories to identify the 2–3 categories representing 80%+ of Scope 3. Prioritise data quality investment there. ESRS E1, BRSR Core, California SB 253, and ISSB S2 all require material Scope 3 disclosure.

📊 Data Quality Hierarchy (GHG Protocol — highest to lowest accuracy)
1
Primary supplier data — actual metered consumption or supplier-disclosed PCF. Highest accuracy. Required for CSRD Cat 1 primary data.
2
Activity-based (average data) — quantity × product-specific EF (e.g. kg of aluminium × 8.24 tCO₂e/t). Good accuracy for physical goods.
3
Distance-based — km × modal EF (transport, commuting, business travel). Moderate accuracy. Requires route or distance data.
4
Spend-based (MRIO) — £/$ spend × economic EF (Exiobase, USEEIO). Use for screening and categories without quantity data. Lower accuracy, suitable for year 1.
5
Hybrid methods — combination of above. Common in practice for Scope 3 Category 1 where some suppliers have PCFs and others do not.

🔢 How to Complete This Inventory — 7 Steps
1
Set the boundary. Choose Operational Control, Financial Control, or Equity Share. Fill in Organisation Details on the Dashboard. This determines which legal entities are included.
2
Collect Scope 1 activity data. Pull metered gas consumption, fuel delivery records, fleet fuel logs, refrigerant service records, and F-Gas log books. Enter in the Scope 1 tab.
3
Collect Scope 2 data — both methods. Pull electricity invoices (kWh per site). Confirm whether renewable energy certificates, PPAs, or GO certificates are held. Enter location-based and market-based separately in the Scope 2 tab.
4
Screen Scope 3 first. Run spend-based estimates for all 15 categories using accounts payable data before entering primary data. This identifies where 80%+ of Scope 3 lives and directs your data collection effort.
5
Enter Scope 3 data in the Scope 3 tab, starting with the highest-priority categories. Use the emission factor guidance in each row to select the correct source and calculation method.
6
Review the Dashboard. Check the Scope 3 share (target: should reflect operational reality — typically 70–90% for manufacturers), intensity metrics, and the framework alignment panel.
7
Export for disclosure. Use CSV export for GRI 305, ESRS E1, BRSR Core, and CDP Climate data entry. Use JSON export to save and resume. Print for board reporting.
